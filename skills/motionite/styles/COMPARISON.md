# NxrStyle comparison

There are 15 house styles, and each one is a complete, opinionated visual system. **Pick exactly one per film.** You may borrow a single technique from another style, but never its whole palette or type system.

## How they differ (prose)

**Energy axis.** 01 Beat Reel, 06 PRESS START and 10 Pixel Trailer are the loud ones: hard cuts on the bar, slams, saturated colour. 13 Clean Launch, 04 Editorial Ink and 12 Console-as-Film are the quiet ones. They lean on restraint, a lot of whitespace, and one meaningful accent. 13 bans glow and overshoot outright, while 04 and 12 allow soft springs and a single italic phrase.

**Camera axis.** Three styles are defined by a single camera move. 05 trucks sideways through one world, 11 zooms in log space through nested worlds, and 12 dollies into a device screen. If your brief is "one continuous shot", choose between these three by direction: sideways (time, journey), inward (hierarchy, "deeper"), or into an object (product reveal).

**Hand-made axis.** 08, 14 and 15 all fake a physical medium, in different ways. 08 is *engraved print* (static hatching, slow push). 14 is *animated paper* (puppets pose on 12s while the camera moves on ones). 15 is *drawing* (lines boil on twos and draw themselves). Never boil textures in any of them.

**Pixel axis.** 06 is a *menu* (UI-like, centred, a blinking prompt) and 10 is a *trailer* (world-first, parallax, bar-grid cuts). Both move on whole pixels, and both need Pixelify Sans or Press Start 2P for legibility at display size.

**UI axis.** 03 floats the real UI in 3D with a giant number. 09 tiles many live mini-UIs into a looping 1:1 poster. 02 doesn't show UI at all; captions carry it. 13 shows UI only as typeset numbers and fragments.

**Format.** 02 is the only native 9:16 style and 09 the only native 1:1. Any other style can be re-framed, but re-plan the layout. Don't crop it.

## Which style for which brief

| If the brief says… | Use | Second choice |
|---|---|---|
| "Hype reel / sizzle / music-driven" | 01 | 10 |
| "Explain this stat for TikTok/Reels/Shorts" | 02 | 15 (re-framed) |
| "Launch our mobile app / payments feature" | 03 | 09 |
| "Thoughtful brand manifesto, essay, investor letter" | 04 | 13 |
| "Show the customer's journey / a day in the life" | 05 | 11 |
| "Retro, playful, gamer audience, 'level up'" | 06 | 10 |
| "Mint / drop / collectible / limited edition" | 07 | 03 |
| "A story, holiday greeting, kids, fable" | 08 | 14 |
| "Social post comparing prices / features" | 09 | 02 |
| "Game trailer / indie game reveal" | 10 | 06 |
| "Zoom from big picture to detail, seamless loop" | 11 | 05 |
| "Physical product, hardware, gadget" | 12 | 03 |
| "Developer tool, infra, API, serious SaaS" | 13 | 04 |
| "Cosy, crafted, warm, music piece" | 14 | 15 |
| "Tutorial, notebook, sketch, 'made by hand'" | 15 | 14 |

Tie-breakers:
1. If the brand ships a style guide, that guide beats every NxrStyle default (palette, font, bans).
2. If there's real footage or real UI, prefer the style whose hero is that asset (03, 10, 12).
3. If the brief needs captions without sound, prefer 02 or 09.
4. Never use the same style for two films in a row for the same client unless asked.
