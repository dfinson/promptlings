# Sources

Reviewed on 2026-11-03. Evidence strength reflects the research base, not how often a claim is repeated.

| Source | Supports | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mayer, *Multimedia Learning* (2nd ed., 2009); Mayer and Johnson (2008), *Journal of Educational Psychology* | Redundancy: do not project text the speaker says. Signaling, segmenting, pre-training, spatial and temporal contiguity | Strong |
| Richter, Scheiter and Eitel (2016), *Educational Research Review* | Signaling meta-analysis | Strong |
| Sweller, van Merriënboer and Paas (1998), *Educational Psychology Review* | Cognitive load, split attention, element interactivity | Strong |
| Alley and Neeley (2005), *IEEE Transactions on Professional Communication*; Garner and Alley (2013), *International Journal of Engineering Education*; [assertion-evidence.com](https://www.assertion-evidence.com/) | Assertion-evidence slides over topic and bullet slides | Moderate to strong |
| Tversky, Morrison and Betrancourt (2002), *International Journal of Human-Computer Studies* | Animation helps only when it carries change that statics cannot; controlled pace | Moderate |
| Höffler and Leutner (2007), *Learning and Instruction* | Animation meta-analysis: small to moderate benefit, larger with learner or presenter control | Moderate |
| Loewenstein (1994), *Psychological Bulletin*; Roediger and Karpicke (2006), *Psychological Science* | Curiosity gaps and retrieval for predict-then-reveal | Moderate to strong |
| Bradbury (2016), *Advances in Physiology Education* | The fixed ten-minute attention span is a myth; vary activity instead | Moderate |
| Pashler et al. (2008), *Psychological Science in the Public Interest* | Learning-style matching is unsupported; do not design for it | Strong (against) |
| [Hohman, Conlen, Heer and Chau, Communicating with Interactive Articles](https://distill.pub/2020/communicating-with-interactive-articles/) | Survey of interactive explanation techniques and evidence | Review |
| [Bret Victor, Explorable Explanations](https://worrydream.com/ExplorableExplanations/), [Ladder of Abstraction](https://worrydream.com/LadderOfAbstraction/), [Media for Thinking the Unthinkable](https://worrydream.com/MediaForThinkingTheUnthinkable/) | Linked representations, scrubbing, showing state rather than asking the audience to simulate it | Practitioner canon |
| [Distill, Research Debt](https://distill.pub/2017/research-debt/) | Moving interpretive labor from audience to author | Practitioner canon |
| [reveal.js speaker view](https://revealjs.com/speaker-view/) | Speaker view requires a local server; notes, timer and preview conventions | Documentation |
| [MDN BroadcastChannel](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/BroadcastChannel), [View Transitions support](https://caniuse.com/view-transitions) | Same-origin sync and browser support | Documentation |
| [GSAP Standard License](https://gsap.com/community/standard-license/) | Free use including former bonus plugins | License |
| [Playwright accessibility testing](https://playwright.dev/docs/accessibility-testing) | Automated axe scans per step | Documentation |
| Duarte, *slide:ology* (2008); Reynolds, *Presentation Zen* (2011) | Projector legibility, restraint, one idea per slide | Practitioner consensus |
