# Technical Demo

Em dashes are banned from all output. Use commas, colons, semicolons, periods, or parentheses instead.

Direct a story the audience can understand, not a feature inventory or repository tour read aloud.

1. **Discover.** Inspect the ask, sources, runtime, existing media and delivery constraints. Establish the audience, problem, why it matters, promised outcome and required proof. Choose an explainer, hands-on demonstration or hybrid. Use source to explain implementation; use actual execution to prove behavior. Never substitute animation for required runtime evidence.
2. **Design.** Propose a short scene sequence with one takeaway per scene: claim, exact source or observed evidence, explanation, focal point and transition. Orient a cold viewer before proof. Introduce important artifacts: what they are, who uses them and when they matter. Preserve source/transcript fidelity and provenance; disclose reconstruction, omissions, time compression and known failures.
3. **Approve.** Explicitly invoke `reasoning-first-narration` before approving the full spoken script and after material script revisions. Critique unsupported claims, lost reasoning, unexplained artifacts, pacing and visual/narration mismatch. Present the story, narration, evidence, assumptions and delivery choices for explicit approval before expensive production. Feedback alone is not approval.
4. **Produce.** For synthetic speech explicitly invoke `tts-setup`, allowing reuse of an accepted setup. Voice, provider, private-text transfer and costs are user decisions. Then invoke `cue-synced-demo` for approved capture, per-line audio, timing and assembly. Use existing specialist tools for synthesis, capture and motion design (`hyperframes` first for HyperFrames work). Keep application flows, selectors and authentication project-local. Rehearse required states and inspect a short real capture before committing to a full take.
5. **Review and revise.** Inspect the final encoded output, scene/event frames, cuts and joins; listen through it with the visuals. Separately assess application correctness, media timing/quality and communication. Reread the evidence for important meaning omitted or oversimplified in every mode. Fix the earliest defective stage, reuse unchanged assets and render from originals after timing changes. Reinvoke narration for material script changes and production for affected media. Reverify the exact final file.
6. **Deliver.** Provide the reviewed video, approved script, timing/check results and rerun instructions. Identify actual limitations, missing human listening or acceptance, and the file verified. Rendering success is not audiovisual acceptance.

Keep secrets, private data, unrelated windows/tabs, account details and notifications out of recordings and service requests. Capture the intended viewport or window at native dimensions, not a full desktop cropped later; do not upscale. Choose captions and mastering for the audience and destination. Stop on failed required evidence, invalid speech or timing failures, and obtain approval before material scope or quality compromises.
